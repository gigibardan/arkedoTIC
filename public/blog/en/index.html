Middle School C++ Guide (Grades 7–8): Fixing Top 10 Errors in if, while & Digit Algorithms

Nine out of ten middle school C++ bugs come from five traps: writing = instead of == inside if(), placing a stray semicolon right after if(...) or while(...), forgetting n = n / 10 inside a digit loop (infinite loop), leaving sums uninitialized instead of int s = 0, and skipping the edge case n = 0.

Kids Online Safety on TikTok, Roblox, Discord & WhatsApp: Stopping Scams, Phishing & Cyberbullying

Most student accounts on Roblox, Discord, or WhatsApp are hijacked through social engineering—fake "Free Robux" pages, compromised WhatsApp "vote for my cousin" links, or shady Discord game downloads—not complex hacking. Turn on 2-Factor Authentication (2FA), inspect the exact domain before signing in, use unique passphrases, and save screenshots if cyberbullying occurs.

Building Your First School Website (Grade 8): Semantic HTML5, HTTPS & Safe Google Sites Publishing

Every modern webpage starts with semantic HTML5 (<!DOCTYPE html>, <head> for metadata like UTF-8 charset and title, and <body> with <header>, <nav>, <main>, and <footer>).
