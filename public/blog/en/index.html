Artificial Intelligence · Grades 6–8 · Teachers · 12 min read

How ChatGPT & LLMs Really Work (Tokens, Softmax & AI Hallucinations): A Practical Guide for Students & Teachers

A Large Language Model (LLM) does NOT think like a human or look up verified encyclopedia facts. It splits text into numeric chunks called tokens (~3–4 characters each) and predicts the most statistically probable next token using Softmax and a Temperature setting. Because it optimizes linguistic plausibility rather than ground truth, an LLM can invent false citations or math answers convincingly (AI hallucinations).

Networks & Safety · Grades 5–8 · Networking · 13 min read

How the Internet Works in 2026: IP, DNS, TCP vs. UDP (Why Gaming Ping Spikes), Ports & Wi-Fi 6/7

The Internet moves data in numbered packets: DNS translates human domain names (arkyedu.com) into numeric IP addresses; TCP guarantees reliable, in-order delivery (websites on HTTPS port 443, downloads), while UDP skips acknowledgments for lowest possible latency (multiplayer gaming, voice/video calls, live streams). For low, steady ping, Ethernet or 5/6 GHz Wi-Fi always beats congested 2.4 GHz.
