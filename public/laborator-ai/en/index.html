What is an AI token and why do LLMs sometimes hallucinate?
LLMs split text into numeric sub-word chunks called tokens and statistically predict the next token. When context is missing or Temperature is high, they may assemble plausible-sounding falsehoods known as hallucinations.
How can students and parents spot an AI Deepfake image or cloned voice scam?
In images: inspect background wall text for gibberish, hands/badges, shadow consistency, and camera EXIF/C2PA data. For audio/video: reject urgent requests for 2FA SMS codes, call back on saved contacts, and use an offline family safe-word.
