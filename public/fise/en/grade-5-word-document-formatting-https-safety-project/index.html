Grade 5 Project & Rubric: Clean School Report Formatting (A4, Typography) & Online Safety

Quick Summary & Key Points:

Tests clean digital typography (no space before commas/periods, single space after, Justify body alignment, A4 PDF export) combined with HTTPS encryption checks and personal privacy rules.

Theory Snapshot: Punctuation rule: never place a space BEFORE a comma or period; always place ONE space AFTER. Export final school reports as .pdf so layout never shifts.

Solve & Auto-Grade (1–10)
1. Part I · 1 (18p)

Which sentence follows standard digital punctuation spacing rules?

  • A) Hello ,today we learn ICT .
  • B) Hello, today we learn ICT.
  • C) Hello,today we learn ICT.
  • D) Hello , today we learn ICT .

Answer & Explanation: B) Hello, today we learn ICT. — Never add a space before a comma or period, and always put one space after a comma.

2. Part I · 2 (18p)

Which paragraph alignment produces straight edges on BOTH left and right margins, like a printed book?

  • A) Align Left
  • B) Center
  • C) Justify
  • D) Align Right

Answer & Explanation: C) Justify — Justify distributes spacing so both left and right paragraph edges align cleanly.

3. Part II · 1 (18p)

What is the name of the encrypted web protocol indicated by the browser padlock (ending with S for Secure)?

Answer & Explanation: HTTPS — HTTPS (HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure) encrypts traffic between your browser and the website server.

4. Part II · 2 (18p)

True or False: It is safe to post your home address and phone number on a public gaming profile as long as you use a cartoon avatar.

  • A) True
  • B) False

Answer & Explanation: B) False — False. Your home address, school, and phone number are private personal data and must never be shared publicly.

5. Part III (18p)

Which key combination exports a Word or Google Docs report into a fixed-layout document ready to print?

  • A) Export / Download as PDF (.pdf)
  • B) Save as .txt
  • C) Rename to .mp3
  • D) Save as .gif

Answer & Explanation: A) Export / Download as PDF (.pdf) — Exporting as PDF (.pdf) locks fonts, images, and margins across all printers and computers.
