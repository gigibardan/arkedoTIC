Grade 5 Graded Test with Rubric: Sequential Algorithms, Flowcharts & Scratch 3.0 (100 pts)

Quick Summary & Key Points:

Complete 100-point exam (90 + 10 pts) covering algorithm properties, standard flowchart shapes, linear variable tracing, and Scratch 3.0 coordinates (X: -240..240, Y: -180..180).

Theory Snapshot: The Scratch stage center is (0, 0). X spans -240 (left) to +240 (right); Y spans -180 (bottom) to +180 (top). Flowchart shapes: Oval = Start/Stop, Parallelogram = Input/Output, Rectangle = Process, Diamond = Decision.

Solve & Auto-Grade (1–10)
1. Part I · 1 (18p)

Which algorithm property guarantees execution stops after a finite number of steps?

  • A) Generality
  • B) Finiteness
  • C) Block color
  • D) Parallelism

Answer & Explanation: B) Finiteness — Finiteness guarantees termination; clarity eliminates ambiguity; generality handles all valid inputs.

2. Part I · 2 (18p)

In a flowchart, which geometric shape represents INPUT or OUTPUT of data?

  • A) Diamond (Decision)
  • B) Rectangle (Process)
  • C) Parallelogram
  • D) Oval

Answer & Explanation: C) Parallelogram — The parallelogram represents Input/Output operations (read / print).

3. Part II · 1 (18p)

Trace the algorithm below and enter the final value of S:

a <- 7
b <- 5
S <- a + b * 2
scriere S

Answer & Explanation: 17 — Following operator precedence: b * 2 = 10, then S = 7 + 10 = 17.

4. Part II · 2 (18p)

A Scratch sprite starts at (x=0, y=0), executes "change x by 60" and "change y by -40". What is its new X coordinate?

Answer & Explanation: 60 — Starting at x = 0, "change x by 60" moves right to x = 60 (while y becomes -40).

5. Part III (18p)

Which Scratch script moves a sprite a total of 100 steps using a loop?

  • A) repeat (5) [ move (10) steps ]
  • B) repeat (4) [ move (25) steps ]
  • C) move (10) steps
  • D) repeat (10) [ turn 90 degrees ]

Answer & Explanation: B) repeat (4) [ move (25) steps ] — 4 iterations × 25 steps = 100 total steps moved forward.
